## What's new

- Newly generated back-off and work-set blocks contain 3–7 sets while keeping every five-column grid filled. Former two-set blocks become seven sets; blocks with three to six sets keep their count. Started workouts and completed history retain their saved prescriptions.
- Choose a lighter Smart workout beside Complete workout before starting your session, and restore the regular workout before recording progress. Relevant recovery explanations appear directly on the Workout screen instead of a separate Smart dialog.
- Screen content adapts its text size and spacing to the available room, with readable scrolling when needed. Dashboard, Program, Statistics and Settings use the bottom navigation instead of repeated screen titles. Content has consistent top and bottom insets, and Program shows Tomorrow without repeating its date.
