## What's new

- Keep daily local backup history for 7, 30 or 90 days, with 30 days as the default. The latest backup remains available. Restore a saved point after a separate confirmation and a verified safety backup of your current data. Automatic cleanup leaves manual exports untouched.
- Beginner routes now alternate training and recovery, with three normal sessions per six route days. Existing cycles retain completed history and started workouts while the remaining plan adopts the new rhythm. The route preview shows the correct training-day period.
- Share larger, centered result cards with your level, cycle and workout number. The download link accompanies the image as separate text. Improve spacing and readability on wider screens.
- On Meet Day, show warm-ups and attempts on separate, centered rows for every lift, without changing the prescribed weights or repetitions.
- Open About & Updates from the header while staying on your current screen.
