Kelani SBD Tracker is a calm, offline-first powerlifting tracker for structured Squat, Bench Press and Deadlift training.

It helps you plan, track and complete SBD workouts without accounts, ads, subscriptions, automatic analytics, telemetry or Kelani cloud sync. Your saved app data stays on your device unless you choose to export or share it. Optional Android calendar integration writes brief workout events to a calendar you select; its provider may sync them.

Smart Training:

Smart Training follows a level-specific 28-workout ideal route through SBD Meet Day. The route remains visible in advance, while confirmed strength determines the prescribed weights.

- Plans heavy, medium and light Squat, Bench Press and Deadlift work for your current level
- Prescribes warm-ups, top work, back-off work and accessories
- Moves the meet closer after Too easy and adds recovery after Too hard or failed work
- Tracks meet readiness without silently replacing route workouts or postponing Meet Day
- Shows past, current and provisional future workouts through the meet and recovery
- All Smart calculations run locally on your device

Features:

- Structured Squat, Bench Press and Deadlift training cycles
- Workout tracking with warm-ups, main work, back-offs and optional accessories
- Automatic progression based on completed training
- Rest timer with audio signals
- Meet Planner for squat, bench press and deadlift attempt selection
- Meet prep checklist for competition-day preparation
- Bodyweight and body composition logging
- 1RM, estimated 1RM and strength statistics
- Perceived effort tracking for sets and completed workouts
- Exercise alternatives for lower-stress training periods
- Local data export and import
- Optional sync of future workouts to a selected Android calendar after you grant calendar access and start the first sync
- Optionally remove Kelani calendar events when workouts are completed in the app
- Workout calendar events include a reminder at the start time; reminders you later change or remove in your calendar are preserved
- Optional anonymous usage summary generated locally and shown in full before you choose whether to share it
- English, Catalan and Dutch interface

Privacy and independence:

- No account required
- No ads
- No automatic analytics or telemetry
- No tracking
- No subscription
- No paywall
- Core training works offline
- Optional Android calendar integration uses calendar read and write permission; your chosen calendar provider may sync its events

Kelani is made for practical long-term strength progress, not social media engagement.
