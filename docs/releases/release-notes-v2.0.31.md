## What's new

- Preview the full provisional Smart route through Meet Day and recovery on the Program screen, with a focused seven-workout view and controls to show the complete route.
- Keep the Program header visible while the workout list scrolls, and keep the current workout centred whenever possible.
- Improve late-cycle taper scheduling for Advanced and Elite athletes by combining the final Bench and Squat session before the last recovery day.
- Make post-meet recovery respond consistently to the meet result: no recovery after Too easy, one day after Optimal, two days after Too hard, and three days after any failed attempt.
- Limit every planned recovery block to a maximum of three consecutive days.
- Preserve the exact weight selected for a later Meet Day attempt after an earlier failed attempt.
- Give failed sets a distinct dark red status colour so they cannot be confused with Squat sets.
