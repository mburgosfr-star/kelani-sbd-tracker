## What's new

- Share a mini-dashboard with your lift records and total, or a richer cycle-results card with 1RM, e1RM, progress and a chart.
- The Dashboard uses larger text and tighter spacing while keeping screen titles consistent.
- Automatic training loads, including accessories and return training, round to 0.5 kg. Automatic meet attempts remain on 2.5 kg increments, and fixed warm-ups remain multiples of 10 kg. Progression rules are unchanged.
- The simplified plate calculator uses a 20 kg barbell and includes microplates, without adding weight for collars.
- Play installations open Google Play for updates, without GitHub update checks or APK download prompts. Free GitHub and IzzyOnDroid installations keep their existing update options. If the installation source cannot be determined, Kelani offers Google Play instead of an APK download.
