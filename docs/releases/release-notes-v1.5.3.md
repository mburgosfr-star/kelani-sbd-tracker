## What's new

- Fixed meet-attempt suggestions that could round to a weight above your actual tested max at light training loads.
- The Dashboard now shows your route to the next meet directly, including the projected window, what's currently the limiting lift, and your projected total — even early in a training cycle, before there's enough data for a firm date.
- Cleaner Dashboard and Workout screens: fewer boxed cards, and animation is now reserved for your current set and current workout, so it's easier to tell what actually needs your attention.
- Balanced workout volume more evenly across multi-lift training days.
- Workout screens are more compact, with a 4-column set/warm-up layout instead of 3.
