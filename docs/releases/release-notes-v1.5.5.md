## What's new

- Meet-attempt suggestions (opener, 2nd, 3rd) are now based on your real, achieved 1RM instead of an estimated one — a lighter training PR still improves your training targets, but no longer inflates your planned meet attempts on its own.
- Laid the groundwork for a more balanced weekly training frequency across Squat, Bench, and Deadlift, so no single lift silently gets ahead of the others over time. Elite-level Deadlift frequency increased from 3 to 4 sessions per week as part of this.
