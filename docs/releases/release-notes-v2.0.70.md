## What's new

- Refine the Smart Bench taper: omit the middle Bench session and remove the final top single. Keep the final work sets at 3 repetitions and the existing intensity, with 2–6 sets filling the workout grid.
- Use consistent warm-up repetitions before singles: 5/3/1 for three warm-ups and 5/5/3/1 for four. Keep the existing warm-up loads.
- Allow manual 0.5 kg adjustments to work sets, accessories and every meet attempt. Display and preserve the chosen loads accurately, including after reloading and restoring backups. Automatic meet percentages and 2.5 kg rounding remain unchanged.
