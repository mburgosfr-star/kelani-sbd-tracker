## What's new

- Smart Training's light days now stay genuinely light: secondary work respects its own intensity ceiling, and failed work triggers recovery without unnecessarily regressing unaffected lifts.
- Heavy Squat and Deadlift progression now keeps moving toward the meet after successful recovery instead of repeating an older intensity, while Bench tapers once its meet goals are demonstrated.
- Weekly lift frequency, heavy/medium/light roles and consecutive-day allowances now follow the athlete-level frequency table more consistently, including the higher Bench and Squat frequencies intended for Advanced and Elite lifters.
- Meet readiness now tracks the real remaining attempt phase for every lift, recognises rounded e1RM-equivalent third-attempt evidence, requires a proper taper and rest before the meet, and estimates the meet window from progression-capable heavy exposures rather than counting every light or medium session as progress.
- Warm-up generation is safer and more practical: lower-body jumps of 60 kg or more are prevented, unnecessary near-work-set warm-ups are removed, plate changes are reduced, and meet-day Squat, Bench and Deadlift use dedicated opener-focused warm-up ladders.
- Meet day now has a focused dashboard with the full attempt plan, opens the workout when tapped, keeps Program view compact, shows exact 90% / 97.5% / 102.5% attempt labels, and replaces general Smart diagnostics with positive execution guidance and the projected total.
- e1RMs are rounded consistently throughout the app, PR badges compare against the real 1RM, and historical 1RM/e1RM charts remain stable and never move downward at the current endpoint.
- Fixed browser storage exhaustion after completing a workout by removing the obsolete duplicate automatic-backup copy; native automatic backups remain intact and their status is shown only where that backup actually exists.
