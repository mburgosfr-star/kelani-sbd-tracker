## What's new

- Celebrate meaningful strength milestones after a workout, including new levels, 1RM and e1RM records, totals, Strength Max, and eStrength Max.
- Show recent Strength Max and eStrength Max records on the dashboard and keep real 1RM strength fully separate from estimated strength.
- Improve Smart Training explanations with consistent intensity wording, cycle-specific e1RM values, and more accurate meet-readiness guidance.
- Generate more balanced warm-ups with controlled weight jumps and consistent barbell-friendly increments.
- Simplify Settings with direct weight-unit selection, current body-data values, and one consolidated About and support section.
- Improve vertical spacing across onboarding, Settings, and completed-workout screens while avoiding unnecessary scrolling when content already fits.
