## What's new

- Keep taper workouts meaningful: heavy top sets now include light back-off work, while light taper work keeps at least three sets of four reps at 60%.
- Make workout feedback clearer with Too easy, Optimal and Too hard labels in every supported language.
- Move the expected meet one day closer after a Too easy workout, using the safest available adjustment while preserving the ideal route.
- Insert exactly one recovery day after a Too hard workout or any number of missed sets, move the expected meet one day later and then resume the original route without a hidden deload or regression.
- Keep meet feedback neutral, while adding one post-meet recovery day for each missed attempt.
- Combine all Too hard outcomes into one count in optional anonymous usage summaries.
