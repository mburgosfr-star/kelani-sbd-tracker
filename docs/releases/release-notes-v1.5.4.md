## What's new

- Fixed a crash right after finishing certain Smart Training workouts that could show a blank screen, and briefly send the app back to onboarding on next launch (your training data was never lost).
- Smart Training's third-attempt phase no longer prescribes a lighter top set than you've already proven — the actual weight now always holds or climbs from your last successful lift.
- Meet-readiness now tracks a third stage after openers and second attempts: the diagnosis keeps pointing at whatever's still needed instead of going quiet once you're close, and training continues appropriately before any taper.
- Cleaned up the Smart diagnosis screen: the readiness gap now always matches the numbers shown above it, and the progress notes are simpler and easier to trust at a glance.
