## What's new

- Avoid single-lift workouts throughout the regular Smart Training route by distributing the same lift frequency and intensity across more complete training days.
- Combine the intermediate taper's medium Squat and heavy Bench work into one session, with a rest day immediately before it.
- Give unavoidable single-lift taper sessions at least three complete set rows, including useful work of at least four reps at 60% or higher alongside the heavy single.
- Keep Too easy route acceleration conservative by removing an optional rest day before removing a planned training session.
