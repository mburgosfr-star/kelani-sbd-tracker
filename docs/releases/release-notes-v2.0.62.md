## What's new

- Use warm-ups based on the first work set or meet opener, with weights in multiples of 10 kg and only 1, 3 or 5 reps. Aim for a final warm-up around 85%, never above 90%. Remove warm-ups above that limit, allowing a larger final jump at very high loads.
- Give five minutes of rest after the last warm-up before a top single, double or triple, including Classic heavy singles. Meet-day rest remains eight minutes.
- Keep the five-column workout grid filled. Work-set and back-off counts adapt to the warm-up count within the existing 1–5 volume-set range. Started workouts and completed history keep their saved prescriptions.
- Keep the current workout visible when expanding or collapsing the Program list.
- Place the desktop notification test and close buttons side by side.
