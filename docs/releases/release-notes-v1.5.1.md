## What's new

- Experience level is now automatic: Smart Training recognizes whether you're Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced or Elite based on your strength ratio and adjusts weekly training frequency accordingly. Tap your level badge on the Dashboard to see your progress.
- Warm-ups are more reliable: never 2 reps, and rep counts never increase as the weight gets heavier.
- Percentages now round to the nearest 5% everywhere, matching the existing 5 kg weight rounding.
- Redesigned the Smart workout info popup for easier reading.
- Clearer visual cues for what's tappable: an animated arrow on the Dashboard, a highlighted current lift in the Program, and an animated plate calculator icon on the active set.
- Fixed a workout list display issue after restoring from backup.
