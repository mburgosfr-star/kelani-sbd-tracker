## What's new

- Set up Kelani in three clear steps: enter your starting strength, choose a training goal, and learn the basics before your first workout.
- Choose Squat priority during onboarding or in Settings when you are at beginner level. Training-focus explanations now match the selected route.
- Follow preparation exercises in training order: preparation for a lift, that lift's warm-ups and work sets, then preparation for the next lift.
- See precise e1RM values consistently in workout results, milestones, and Smart Training explanations, including when older workout summaries contain rounded estimates.
