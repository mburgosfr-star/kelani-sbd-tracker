## What's new

- Fixed Smart Training sometimes suggesting another workout instead of rest when every lift had already reached its weekly training limit.
- Smart Training can now schedule a genuine 3-lift day (e.g. Deadlift + Bench + Squat) when all three lifts are due and you're well recovered, shown with a short title like "DBS".
- Balanced workout volume more evenly across multi-lift training days.
- Workout screens are now more compact, with a 4-column set/warm-up layout instead of 3.
- Smaller Dashboard layout and workout-info text fixes.
