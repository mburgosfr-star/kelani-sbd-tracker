## What's new

- Milestone share cards have a new design. The shared dashboard shows recent record gains.
- Onboarding and Settings are easier to read. The dashboard shows new records next to the corresponding values.
- Program shows the full training route, starting near your current workout.
- Statistics shades the gap between 1RM and e1RM.
- When an exercise has up to five sets, its warm-ups and work sets share one row. Accessory sets are centered below the exercise name.
- After you complete a rest day, a photo with a check mark confirms it.
- On Android, automatic backup retries use a fresh temporary file, and manual exports can be saved directly to a chosen folder and shared afterwards. Backup History shows the details for one selected recovery point.
